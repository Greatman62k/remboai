How it works

Three steps between a message and money in the till.

  1. The customer writes like a person

    "Fade kesho mchana", a voice note in Sheng, a photo of braids. RemboAI understands the service, the day, and the language, then offers real free slots with named stylists.

  2. The deposit locks the chair

    Daraja-enabled tills get an automatic M-Pesa prompt. A manual till uses a guided payment claim that the owner confirms with one tap. Pasted SMS screenshots never confirm a booking on their own.

  3. Reminders and the waitlist do the follow-up

    24-hour, 2-hour, and 3-hour nudges with Keep or Cancel buttons. A cancellation, or a silence, hands the freed slot to the next waiting customer in seconds.

Questions

Asked by owners like you.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. Everything happens in the WhatsApp or Telegram they already use. The invite link opens a chat, not a download page.

My salon uses a manual till without Daraja. Does that work?

Yes. Configure it with only the bare seven-digit till number. The customer pays there and sends the amount plus M-Pesa code in chat; you confirm the claim with one tap and the booking locks in. Screenshots alone never confirm anything.

What happens when someone cancels?

The freed slot is offered to your waitlist in join order. The first to tap Yes within 10 minutes takes it. If nobody is waiting, the slot simply opens up.

What if a customer ignores the reminders?

The 3-hour Still-coming nudge carries Keep or Cancel buttons. A Cancel frees the slot at once; silence close to the appointment releases it toward the waitlist, and the customer is told.

Can two customers book the same chair?

No. The calendar holds a slot the moment it is tapped and the database refuses overlaps, so even two simultaneous taps cannot double-book one stylist.
