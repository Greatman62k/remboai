Setup guide

Booking conversations, with the diary kept in code.

RemboAI is open-source booking software for barbershops and salons. Its backend includes Telegram and WhatsApp channel integrations, language-model assistance, and application-enforced booking and payment rules. A running service requires an operator-configured deployment and provider accounts.

  • Customers interact through the messaging channel configured by the operator.
  • Payment flows support configured M-PESA STK and owner-reviewed manual payment claims.
  • Cancellations can offer released slots to the salon's waitlist.

How it works

A message becomes a booking through verified application rules.

  1. The customer writes like a person

    "Fade kesho mchana", a Sheng voice note, or a photo of a haircut style. RemboAI understands the service, the day, and the language, then offers real free slots with your team.

  2. The deposit locks the chair

    Where configured, Daraja can send an M-PESA prompt. Manual M-PESA and Airtel Money routes give customers payment instructions; the owner checks each claim before confirming the payment.

  3. Reminders and the waitlist do the follow-up

    Configured 24-hour and 2-hour reminders, a 3-hour Keep or Cancel check, and a 30-minute ETA for the customer and owner. A customer cancellation frees the slot for the waitlist; silence leaves the confirmed booking in place.

For owners

Run the whole diary from your own chat.

Owner commands and business setup are available through supported chat workflows.

Set up by chatting, not by forms

Type /start register and answer a few questions: shop name, services with prices (typed or as a voice note), opening hours. Your barbershop or salon gets its own invite link. Example only: t.me/Remboai1_bot?start=your-shop; the real link uses your shop's slug.

Diary commands in plain words

See today's bookings, block a day off, cancel or move a booking, mark no-shows, and shift the diary when running late. The application sends notifications to affected customers through the configured channel.

Never double-booked, never overcharged

A PostgreSQL constraint prevents overlapping active bookings for a stylist. Daraja transactions use callback and reconciliation paths; manual payment claims wait for owner review. Refund-review items remain visible to the owner.

Under the hood

Meta's models understand. Code guarantees.

Model assistance is bounded by application rules

When agent mode is enabled, the configured model can interpret a request and propose tool actions. Application code validates the actions and checks customer-facing claims against business facts. Deterministic flows remain available when model output is unavailable or rejected. Voice and image handling depend on configured ASR and vision services.

Language

Sheng in, Swahili out. English welcome too.

Customers write however they speak. Voice notes are transcribed, photos are matched to services, and replies come back in the customer's own language. Returning customers hear "Karibu tena".

Questions

Asked by owners like you.

Do my customers need to install anything?

Customers use the messaging channel configured for the deployment. Telegram and WhatsApp adapters are present in the code; live availability depends on bot setup, account verification, credentials, and deployment configuration.

Can customers pay without an M-PESA prompt?

Yes. During setup, choose a seven-digit M-PESA till or a phone destination identified as Pochi la Biashara, direct M-PESA Send Money, or Airtel Money. Customers receive matching instructions and send the transaction reference in chat. You confirm after checking your account. A till is entered as only its seven digits.

What happens when someone cancels?

The freed slot is offered to your waitlist in join order. The first to tap Yes within 10 minutes takes it. If nobody is waiting, the slot simply opens up.

What if a customer ignores the reminders?

The 3-hour Still-coming check carries Keep or Cancel buttons. A Cancel frees the slot at once and tells the owner; if the customer does not reply, the booking stays confirmed. The customer and owner receive an ETA 30 minutes before the appointment.

Can two customers book the same chair?

No. The calendar holds a slot the moment it is tapped and the database refuses overlaps, so even two simultaneous taps cannot double-book one stylist.

What happens if the AI model is unavailable?

When agent mode is enabled but model output is unavailable or rejected, supported requests can fall back to deterministic booking flows. Availability depends on the configured channel and application state.

Where does my customers' data go?

Model-assisted messages, voice notes, and images are sent to the configured external providers. RemboAI stores booking and operational records in its backend; retention varies by record type and configuration. Operators should review the deployment's retention settings and provider terms before using real customer data.

Is RemboAI open source?

Yes, RemboAI is open source under the MIT licence. Read the code on GitHub.
