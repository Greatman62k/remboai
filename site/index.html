How it works

Three steps between a message and money in your account.

  1. The customer writes like a person

    "Fade kesho mchana", a voice note in Sheng, a photo of braids. RemboAI understands the service, the day, and the language, then offers real free slots with named stylists.

  2. The deposit locks the chair

    Where configured, Daraja sends an M-PESA prompt. Owners can also choose a seven-digit M-PESA till, Pochi la Biashara, direct M-PESA Send Money, or Airtel Money. The owner checks each manual transfer claim before confirming the booking.

  3. Reminders and the waitlist do the follow-up

    Configured 24-hour and 2-hour reminders, a 3-hour Keep or Cancel check, and a 30-minute ETA for the customer and owner. A customer cancellation frees the slot for the waitlist; silence leaves the confirmed booking in place.

Questions

Asked by owners like you.

Do my customers need to install anything?

Customers who already use Telegram can book there now without installing anything else. The current invite link opens our Telegram bot. WhatsApp will be available after Meta completes our business verification.

Can customers pay without an M-PESA prompt?

Yes. During setup, choose a seven-digit M-PESA till or a phone destination identified as Pochi la Biashara, direct M-PESA Send Money, or Airtel Money. Customers receive matching instructions and send the transaction reference in chat. You confirm after checking your account. A till is entered as only its seven digits.

What happens when someone cancels?

The freed slot is offered to your waitlist in join order. The first to tap Yes within 10 minutes takes it. If nobody is waiting, the slot simply opens up.

What if a customer ignores the reminders?

The 3-hour Still-coming check carries Keep or Cancel buttons. A Cancel frees the slot at once and tells the owner; if the customer does not reply, the booking stays confirmed. The customer and owner receive an ETA 30 minutes before the appointment.

Can two customers book the same chair?

No. The calendar holds a slot the moment it is tapped and the database refuses overlaps, so even two simultaneous taps cannot double-book one stylist.
