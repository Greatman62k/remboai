Setup guide

Register by chatting. Take bookings today.

No forms, no portals, no computer needed. Registration and bookings are live on Telegram today. WhatsApp will be available after Meta completes business verification.

1. Start registration

Open the bot and send /start register, or simply write what you want: "I want to register my business" or "Nataka kusajili salon yangu". Either one starts the same walkthrough.

You: Nataka kusajili salon yangu
RemboAI: Karibu! Jina la salon yako ni lipi?

2. Name your shop and yourself

Give the business name and confirm you are the owner. If your name closely resembles another salon's name, a twin check runs first, so customers never land at the wrong shop.

3. Send your services and prices

Type the list ("Braids 1500, Silk press 2500"), or send a voice note saying them, or photograph your price board and type the list alongside it. Then confirm the list and set how long each service takes.

You: [Voice note] "Braids elfu moja mia tano, silk press elfu mbili mia tano"
RemboAI: Nimepata: Braids 1500, Silk press 2500. Ni sawa?

4. Set your hours and go live

Give your opening hours and deposit. If you charge a deposit, choose a seven-digit M-PESA till or a phone number and identify it as Pochi la Biashara, direct M-PESA Send Money, or Airtel Money. For a phone route, provide the number where the salon receives those payments; no separate owner contact number is needed. Review everything and confirm. Your shop then goes live with its own invite link. Example only: t.me/Remboai1_bot?start=your-shop; the real link uses your salon's slug.

5. Share the link, take bookings

Share your Telegram invite link in your WhatsApp status, your bio, and on a small printed card at the mirror. Today it opens your salon in Telegram; WhatsApp support will follow after Meta completes business verification. Registration attempts are limited to a few per person per day, so a stranger cannot flood the diary with fake shops.

Choose the payment path that fits your salon.

Set your manual destination during chat registration: a seven-digit M-PESA till, a Pochi la Biashara number, a direct M-PESA Send Money number, or an Airtel Money number. Tell us which service uses a phone destination so customers see the right instructions. They pay your selected destination and send a transaction reference; you verify each manual claim before confirming. For automatic M-PESA prompts, Daraja must be configured separately.
